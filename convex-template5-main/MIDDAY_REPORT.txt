TASK 4: INDEPENDENT PAYROLL CALCULATION & LOP BASE RECONCILIATION
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Status: COMPLETED (Verified & Mathematically Reconciled)
Core Engine Reference: packages/backend/convex/statutory.ts & packages/backend/convex/salaries.ts

Mathematical Formulation & Rules Audited:
  1. Gross Salary Composition:
     Gross = Basic Pay + HRA + Special Allowance + Conveyance + Medical + Other Allowances
     (Reimbursements are non-taxable expense settlements and excluded from gross earnings).

  2. Loss of Pay (LOP) Deduction Base:
     The daily deduction rate is derived dynamically based on employer workspace configuration:
       - Mode A (Fixed 30 Days):
         dailyRate = grossSalary / 30
       - Mode B (Calendar Days):
         dailyRate = grossSalary / totalDaysInCurrentMonth (e.g., 28, 29, 30, or 31)
     
     Loss of Pay Deduction:
       lopDeduction = Math.round(lopDays * dailyRate)
     Earned Gross Salary:
       earnedGross = Math.max(0, grossSalary - lopDeduction)

  3. Statutory Contributions & Ceilings:
     • Employee Provident Fund (EPF):
       - Wage Base = Basic + DA (capped at statutory limit ₹15,000 / month, or ₹25,000 if configured).
       - Employee Contribution = 12% of EPF Base.
       - Employer Contribution = 3.67% EPF + 8.33% EPS (capped at ₹1,250) + EDLI & admin fees.
     • Employee State Insurance (ESIC):
       - Applicable only if Gross Salary <= ₹21,000 / month.
       - Employee Contribution = 0.75% of Gross; Employer Contribution = 3.25% of Gross.
     • Professional Tax (PT):
       - State-specific graduated slabs (e.g., Karnataka: ₹200/month for gross >= ₹25,000).
     • Tax Deducted at Source (TDS):
       - Sourced from active financial year declarations, projected annual earnings, and regime.

  4. Net Take-Home Salary Reconciliation:
     Total Deductions = lopDeduction + statutoryDeductions (EPF + ESIC + PT + TDS) 
                      + activeLoanEMIs + voluntaryDeductions
     Net Salary = Math.max(0, Math.round(grossSalary - TotalDeductions) + approvedReimbursements)

  5. Independent Verification Benchmark:
     - Given Employee Baseline:
       * Basic: ₹50,000 | HRA: ₹25,000 | Special Allowance: ₹25,000 -> Gross = ₹100,000
       * LOP Days: 0 (or 1 day @ ₹100,000/30 = ₹3,333.33)
       * EPF: ₹1,800 (12% of ₹15,000 ceiling)
       * PT: ₹200 (Karnataka slab)
       * TDS (projected): ₹5,000
       * Loan EMI: ₹0
       * Approved Reimbursements: ₹0
       * Expected Net Pay = ₹100,000 - (₹1,800 + ₹200 + ₹5,000) = ₹93,000.00
     - Multi-Lifecycle Partial Payment Reconciliation:
       * Payment 1 (Partial): ₹50,000.00 (Recorded with Bank UTR reference)
       * Remaining Balance Computed: ₹43,000.00 (Exact, preventing double-credit)
       * Payment Lifecycle: PARTIALLY_PAID
       * Payment 2 (Final Settlement): ₹43,000.00 (Recorded with Settlement Reference)
       * Final Status Transition: PAID (Remaining Balance: ₹0.00)
